Hi! I'm Sarah, the creator behind MODUS. With a passion for minimalist living and technology, I started this blog to share my journey and insights on how to lead a balanced digital lifestyle. Through MODUS, I aim to inspire others to embrace simplicity while staying connected in our tech-driven world.

On this blog, I share insights, experiences, and practical guides on digital decluttering, mindful technology use, and building sustainable productivity systems. My writing is grounded in real-world experimentation and a deep belief that less can truly be more when it comes to our digital lives.

When I'm not writing, you'll find me practicing meditation, reading philosophy, or exploring minimalism in other areas of life. I believe that simplicity is the ultimate sophistication, and I'm constantly learning how to apply this principle more deeply.
